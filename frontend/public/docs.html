Projects

Every job lives here — from the first enquiry to the final payment. A project holds the customer, vehicle or property, measurements, appointments, quote, invoice, payments, files, notes and messages.

Menu → Projects · /projects
Why it exists: one record per job means nobody asks "where is the quote?" or "did they pay the deposit?" — it is all on the same page.

1Projects list

  • Table or card view, search (name, number, rego, address…), filters for stage, segment, source and archived, plus the global store switcher.
  • Sort columns, see totals, and Export CSV.

2Create a new project (New lead)

  1. Pick the segment: AutomotiveResidentialCommercial.
  2. Choose an existing customer or type a new one (name, phone, email…).
  3. Automotive: year, make, model, body type, colour, rego. Residential/commercial: address, glazing type, orientation.
  4. Store, owner, source (Website, Google, Referral…), priority, tags and notes → Create.
New lead: "Liam Walker · 2024 Toyota Hilux · body type Dual-cab ute". Because the body type is set, TotalOps automatically fills the measurement sheet with the Hilux glass sizes (front doors, rear doors, rear cab glass). The project gets a number such as PRJ-1072.

3Project stages

New lead→Consultation→Measurements→Proposal→Approval→Scheduled→In progress→Invoicing→Job closed

Click a stage in the stepper at the top of a project to move it. TotalOps checks the rules on the server, so jobs can't skip important steps:

To move to…You first need…
Proposal or laterA proposal created
ApprovalThe proposal sent to the customer
ScheduledAn installation appointment booked
In progressInstaller checked in to the installation
InvoicingInstallation completed (or invoice created)
Job closedInstallation complete and paid in full
Automatic stage movesYou rarely need to move stages by hand: sending a proposal moves to Proposal, customer approval to Approval, booking an installation to Scheduled, check-in to In progress, completion to Invoicing, and the final payment closes the job.
No going backOnce a job is Scheduled it can't move to an earlier stage, and an invoiced job can't move before Invoicing.

4Inside a project — the 7 tabs

Overview

A "next move" suggestion, vehicle/property and location, film & shade picker, special requirements, the next appointment and an activity timeline of everything that happened.

Appointments

Book consultation, measurement, installation, site visit or collection. Pick date, start/end, store, lead installer (only installers of that store), co-installers, bay, in-shop/on-site, address, dealer, notes and reminder. Actions: check in, on the way, complete, reopen, remove, and shop status.

Double-booking protectionIf the installer or the bay is already booked at that time you get a clear message, e.g. "Jake Reynolds is already booked 09:00–11:00 on 2026-11-02 (PRJ-1005)".

Measurements

  • Areas (rooms / zones) with panes: label, window type or vehicle zone, width, height, unit (mm, cm, m, in, ft), quantity, film, include tick.
  • Load vehicle template for 8 body styles, plus optional windscreen visor strip and sunroof.
  • Voice measure: tap the mic and say it — the pane is added automatically.
  • Installer checklist, a Verified switch, notes and CSV export.
  • Cut plan: shows how the panes fit on the roll, linear metres and waste %.
  • Smart recommend: runs the Smart Advisor on this sheet and lets you apply the best film.
Voice: "Master bedroom, window 1, 34 and a half by 60 inches" → room Master bedroom, pane Window 1, 34.5 × 60 in, qty 1.
Voice: "Quarter glass, 350 by 250 mm, 2 panes" → 350 × 250 mm, qty 2.

Quote & invoice

Build the customer offer (see Proposals for how options work), then create the invoice, print it, send it, and record deposits/payments.

  • Start from package — copy a saved proposal package.
  • Price from measurements — creates lines from the measured m² × film sell price.
  • Send — creates the customer review link and logs an email (simulated).
  • Customer review link — create, copy, open, or withdraw it.
  • Record simulated approval — when the customer approves by phone or in person.

Install & notes

Install progress (pending → in progress → complete), check-in / complete buttons, plus notes and tasks for this job.

Files & photos

Drag-and-drop photos and documents (before/after photos, signed forms). Preview, download or delete.

Messages

SMS-style thread with the customer. Use a template or type freely; variables like {{first_name}} are filled in. Simulate customer reply is for testing.

Edit project details · Edit customer contact · Send customer message · Copy project link · Duplicate · Archive / Restore · Delete permanently.

3D Tint Visualizer

Show customers their car in 3D with tint on every window — the exact film and shade, legal check included — before they book.

Menu → 3D Visualizer · /visualizer
Why it exists: customers find it hard to imagine "20% vs 35%". Seeing their own car (and the legal limit) closes the sale faster and avoids comebacks.

1Viewer controls

  • Camera: Front ¾SideRearTopInside — Inside shows the view from the driver's seat looking out.
  • Light: DaySunsetNightStudio and Rotate. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
  • Badge top-right: Legal in AU-WA or a red warning if any window is too dark.
  • Cards below: average solar heat rejected, estimated parked-cabin cooling and the region rule.

2Tint tab

  • Quick packages: Factory clear, Legal max (darkest legal ceramic), Privacy, Heat shield 70, Limo 5%.
  • Per zone — front side windows, rear side windows & quarters, rear windscreen, sunroof: choose film and shade. The chip shows the combined VLT and the legal minimum, e.g. 85% / min 35%.
  • Windscreen visor strip on/off (sized from the region rule, e.g. top 10%).
How "combined VLT" worksGlass already blocks some light. Combined VLT = film VLT × factory glass. Example: a 35% film on 85% front glass = 29.75% combined — below WA's 35% front minimum, so TotalOps marks it red.

3Vehicle tab

8 body types (sedan, hatch, SUV, wagon, dual-cab ute, single-cab ute, coupe, van), paint palette or custom colour, finish (gloss, metallic, pearl, matte), black roof, chrome delete, wheel colour and size, factory privacy rear glass, sunroof.

4Photo AI tab

  1. Upload a photo of the customer's car.
  2. The paint colour is detected in the browser and applied to the 3D car automatically.
  3. If AI is enabled, TotalOps also detects make, model, year, body type, finish, wheels and privacy glass, and outlines the windows.
  4. Tint preview on the photo: windows (from AI or drawn by clicking points) are filled with the chosen film; drag the before/after slider.
  5. Optional Generate real 3D mesh turns the photo into a 3D model.
Needs setupAI detection needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and the real 3D mesh needs MESHY_API_KEY in backend/.env.local. Without them, colour detection and manual window outlines still work. Per-window tint is not available on a generated mesh.

5Design tab

Snapshot downloads a PNG. Save design stores the setup (and picture) so you can load it later or attach it to a project.

Customer with a white RAV4 in the shop: upload their photo → car turns white SUV in 3D → press Legal max → switch to Inside and Night to show visibility → Save design and attach it to their project.
AI Studio

Smart Film Advisor

Tell it the job and the customer's goals. It ranks every film, picks the darkest legal shade per window, chooses roll widths, plans the cuts and prices the job.

Menu → Smart Advisor · /advisor
Why it exists: choosing the right film and roll size takes experience and maths. The Advisor makes every staff member quote like your best installer — and stops illegal or risky installs.

1Inputs

  • Segment: automotive or flat glass (residential / commercial).
  • Automotive: body type, factory privacy rear glass, visor strip, sunroof, legal region.
  • Flat glass: a panes table (label, width, height, unit, qty) or Load from project; glass type (single clear, tinted, double glazed, Low-E, laminated, toughened), orientation (N, E, S, W…), climate, partial shading.
  • Goals 0–5: heat, privacy, UV, glare, security, clarity, budget. Plus old-film removal and "signal-safe only".

2What it calculates

OutputMeaning
Best match + scoreFilm with the highest score (0–100) for your goals, with reasons and warnings.
Legal shade per zoneDarkest shade that is still legal on each window after allowing for factory glass.
Thermal stressLow / medium / high risk of glass cracking on home glass (dark film on double-glazed, Low-E, large west-facing panes…).
Per-pane tableCut size with trim, the narrowest roll width that fits, or the number of drops if a seam is needed.
Cut planPieces packed on the roll: linear metres, film m² and waste %.
PricingSell price, material cost, labour hours/cost and margin %.
Stock checkWhether you have enough of that film in stock at the store.
SavingsHeat blocked, kWh and $ per year, CO₂ and payback (homes), or cabin temperature drop (cars).
Input: SUV, Western Australia, goals heat 5 / privacy 4.
Result: "Run 50% on the front sides, 20% on the rear sides, 20% on the rear windscreen — the darkest legal combination. 3.09 m² of glass, cut from a 1016 mm roll using 4.21 linear m (28% waste). Parked cabin up to ~9°C cooler."
Input: home lounge, two 2400×2100 mm panes + one door, double-glazed, west-facing, goals heat 5, clarity 4.
Result: spectrally selective film recommended, thermal stress medium (confirm with manufacturer), ≈1,475 kWh / $472 saved per year.
ActionsApply to project sets the film on the loaded project. Open in 3D shows the same film on the car in the visualizer.

Film Library

Your catalogue of window films with their real performance data. The Advisor, the 3D Visualizer, inventory and quotes all read from here.

Menu → Film Library · /films
Why it exists: correct film data = correct legal checks, recommendations and prices everywhere else.
  • Cards with shade swatches, filters (category, segment), search and a show-inactive switch.
  • Create / edit: name, brand, line, category, segments, description, IRR, UVR, reflectivity, signal-safe, tint colour, thickness, warranty, roll widths, cost and sell per m², glass compatibility and the shades table.
  • Compare up to 3 films side by side.

Glossary

TermPlain meaning
VLTVisible Light Transmission — how much light gets through. 5% = very dark, 70% = almost clear.
TSERTotal Solar Energy Rejected — overall heat blocked (higher is cooler).
IRRInfrared rejection — the heat you feel on your skin.
UVRUV rejection — protects skin and stops interiors fading (usually 99%).
GlareHow much glare is reduced.
AbsorptanceHow much solar energy the film soaks up. High values raise thermal-stress risk on some home glass.
ReflectivityMirror effect. Automotive film over 10% is flagged.
Glass compatibilityok / caution / no per glass type. "no" removes the film from Advisor results for that glass.
Delete ruleFilms used by inventory rolls or projects can't be deleted — mark them inactive.

Proposals

All quotes in one place — drafts, sent, viewed and approved — with the customer approval link.

Menu → Proposals · /proposals
Why it exists: instead of one fixed price, customers choose between options online. Choice raises approval rates and average job value.

1The proposals page

  • Tiles: drafts, awaiting customer, approved value, win rate. Search and segment filter.
  • Board view: drag between Draft → Sent → Viewed. Dropping on Approved opens the "Record approval" form.
  • List view with total, expiry, last activity, open project and copy customer link.

2How offer options work

TypeRuleExample
AlternativesOptions with the same group name — the customer picks one.Group "Tint level": Ceramic IR $520 Recommended or Carbon $330
Add-onsOptions with no group — the customer can tick any."Old film removal" $120

Each option has line items (from the service catalogue or custom), a discount and an optional "comparison price" shown crossed out. Proposal settings: intro, footer, terms, tax %, valid until, deposit (none / fixed / %), message after approval and a next-step button.

Customer picks Ceramic IR ($520) + Removal ($120) = $640 + 10% GST = $704, deposit 50% = $352. Alternatives in the same group are never added together.

3Customer review link

When you Send a proposal, the customer gets a private page (no login) showing your logo, intro, measured scope and option cards. They choose options, see the live total and deposit, type their name, tick "I acknowledge" and press Approve.

  • Opening the link marks the proposal Viewed.
  • Approving marks it Approved, updates the project value and stage, and triggers automations.
  • Expired links ask the customer to contact you. A withdrawn link shows "This link has been withdrawn".
Locked after invoicingOnce an invoice exists, the quote can't be changed. Duplicate the project for a revised scope.

Operations

Reports

Analytics for the whole business, plus CSV/JSON exports.

Menu → Reports · /reports
Why it exists: to answer "which store, film and installer makes us the most money?" with numbers, not gut feel.
  • Range 30 / 90 / 365 days / all time; the store switcher applies.
  • Tiles: pipeline value, won value, closed jobs, invoiced, collected, outstanding, measured m², stock on hand, average job value, win rate.
  • Charts (switch to Tables to see the numbers): projects by stage, by segment, by store (invoiced vs pipeline), conversion funnel (leads → proposals → approved → closed), film usage.
  • Installer leaderboard: completed jobs, hours, value, rating.
  • Exports: projects, contacts, stock, payments, schedule (CSV) and a full JSON backup.
Funnel shows 60 leads → 40 proposals → 12 approved. Approval is the weak step, so you turn on the "Proposal follow-up" automation and add a cheaper alternative option to your packages.
